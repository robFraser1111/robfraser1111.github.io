LumenDesk privacy policy

Effective 19 September 2026. Operator: Rob Fraser (Australia), publishing as LumenDesk. Not affiliated with Signify N.V., Philips Hue, or related trademarks.

What LumenDesk is

LumenDesk is a Windows desktop controller for Philips Hue lights on your local network. Core control talks to your Hue Bridge on the LAN. It does not require a Hue or Signify account, and it does not use Hue cloud remote for day‑to‑day control.

Data that stays on your device

Connection details such as your Bridge IP address, the local Hue app key used to authenticate with the Bridge, and app preferences are stored on your PC. We do not operate a LumenDesk cloud account system or user database.

Local network

On the same Wi‑Fi / LAN, LumenDesk sends control commands to your Hue Bridge. That traffic stays on your network. We do not proxy those commands through our servers.

What we do not do

No LumenDesk accounts, no ads, no selling personal information, no tracking you across other apps or sites. We do not sell your personal data.

Crash and error reporting

When crash reporting is enabled, LumenDesk may send crash, error, and diagnostic reports via Sentry. Reports are used only to diagnose and fix reliability issues. They do not include advertising identifiers, and we do not sell that data. The Sentry DSN bundled with the app is a public client key, not a secret. Sentry may receive technical details such as OS version, app version, and stack traces; we scrub sensitive fields where practical. See Sentry’s privacy policy for how they process that data on our behalf.

Children

LumenDesk is a home lighting controller, not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
